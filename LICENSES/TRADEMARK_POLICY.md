## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may use the word “CraftVia,” in plain text and in a truthful manner, to:

- refer to the CraftVia project or official CraftVia materials;
- state that a product is compatible with, designed for, or based on CraftVia;
- link to or discuss the CraftVia project;
- accurately identify unmodified official design files or hardware manufactured exactly from an identified official release; or
- make factually accurate statements in news, commentary, education, research, and reviews.

These uses must not suggest sponsorship, certification, endorsement, official status, or affiliation that does not exist.

Prior written permission from Kohei Ikeda is required to:

- offer for sale, sell, commercially distribute, or include in a paid product or service any hardware bearing a CraftVia Mark, including a mark embedded in unmodified official manufacturing data;

## Hardware made from official design files

Open hardware rights allow hardware to be manufactured, modified, and sold in accordance with the applicable hardware license. They do not grant rights to the CraftVia Marks and do not authorize anyone to present third-party hardware as manufactured, quality-controlled, warranted, certified, or sold by CraftVia.

If an official release includes a CraftVia Mark in its Gerber or other manufacturing data, you may reproduce that embedded mark without separate permission only as part of manufacturing an unmodified copy of that identified release for personal, internal, educational, research, evaluation, or other non-commercial use. This includes ordering the hardware from a third-party fabrication service acting solely as a contract manufacturer on the customer's instructions, provided that the fabrication service does not market or offer the hardware as its own CraftVia-branded catalogue product.

Prior written permission is required to offer for sale, sell, commercially distribute, or include in a paid product or service any hardware bearing a CraftVia Mark. This requirement applies even when the mark is embedded in unmodified official manufacturing data.

This trademark requirement applies only to the CraftVia Marks. It does not restrict manufacture, modification, or commercial sale of the hardware design under the applicable open hardware license when the CraftVia Marks are removed, all applicable license and copyright notices are retained, and the product is not presented as an official CraftVia product. A truthful plain-text compatibility or attribution statement may still be used.

For modified designs and commercial products that do not have written trademark permission, remove CraftVia logos and other source-identifying artwork where reasonably practicable. You may use a truthful plain-text statement such as:

> Based on the CraftVia Core-S G031 v1 rev0 design. Modified and manufactured independently by [name]. Not an official CraftVia product.

Statements such as “compatible with CraftVia,” “for use with CraftVia,” and “based on CraftVia” are permitted when accurate. Use the CraftVia name only as much as needed to communicate compatibility, and make your own product or organization name more prominent.
